### Slide 2: Main Problem 1 — Route Checklist & Fuel
* **Title:** **Riders leave Barangay routes unchecked, doubling fuel use.**
* **3 Short Points:**
  * **Unchecked Checklist:** Riders forget or cannot explain why assigned Barangays are unchecked.
  * **Operator Confirmation:** Riders must manually comply with the operator to verify missed routes.
  * **Extra Fuel Wasted:** Forgotten traverses force a second delivery trip when customers re-order.
* **Editable Chart:** *Fuel Used per Barangay Route (1st Trip vs. Repeat Trip)*
* **Say:** *"Problem 1: Riders often forget why a Barangay in their route pattern is left unchecked on the checklist, requiring manual confirmation with the operator. When they forget to traverse a Barangay, customers re-order and the rider drives back a second time—wasting fuel."*

### Slide 3: Main Problem 2 — 2-Hour Manual Auditing
* **Title:** **Manual row-by-row auditing takes 2 hours every day.**
* **3 Short Points:**
  * **Row-by-Row Checking:** Operator manually checks every pickup and delivery customer row.
  * **Manual Math:** Counts how many gallons each customer got and computes total cost by hand.
  * **2 Hours of Work Daily:** 120 minutes lost every day just reconciling customer rows.
* **Editable Chart:** *Daily Audit Time: 120 Mins (Manual) vs. 5 Mins (AquaLedger)*
* **Say:** *"Problem 2: Hard auditing. When pickup customers pay, the operator manually monitors row by row per customer to calculate how many gallons they got and the total cost. This takes **2 full hours of manual work** every day."*

### Slide 4: Bonus Problem — 1–2 Week Gap & Lent Gallons
* **Title:** **1-to-2-week buying gaps expose lent gallons to competitors.**
* **3 Short Points:**
  * **Exclusive Gallon Deal:** LG lends free gallons in exchange for customer exclusivity.
  * **1–2 Week Refill Gap:** Versatile buying patterns hide customers who don't buy for 1–2 weeks.
  * **Behind-the-Back Refills:** Suspicious idle borrowers may be refilling LG's gallons at competitors.
* **Editable Chart:** *Customer Holding 8 Lent Gallons (Days 1–14 Refill Drop-Off)*
* **Say:** *"Bonus Problem: LG lends gallons in exchange for exclusive refills. Because buying patterns vary, some customers don't buy for 1 to 2 weeks—which is suspicious and means they may be stabbing LG behind its back by refilling at another station."*

---

### Slide 5: The Solution — `AquaLedger`
* **Title:** **AquaLedger fixes route checks, row audits, and gallon tracking.**
* **3 Columns:**
  1. **Barangay Route Gate:** Riders must submit photo proof or a reason code before closing a Barangay—stopping missed traverses and repeat fuel trips.
  2. **Auto Row Calculator:** Auto-calculates gallons and total cost per customer row—cutting 2 hours of auditing down to 5 seconds.
  3. **14-Day Idle Alert:** Tracks Days 1–31 buying patterns and flags any customer who hasn't refilled in 1–2 weeks for gallon pull-out.
* **Say:** *"Our solution, **AquaLedger**, solves all three: (1) Barangay Route Gate stops missed routes and extra fuel, (2) Auto Row Calculator turns the 2-hour row audit into 5 seconds, and (3) 14-Day Idle Alert flags customers who don't buy for 1–2 weeks to protect lent gallons."*
