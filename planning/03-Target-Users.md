# Target Users: Who We Are Building For

## Overview
The **LG Nature's Water** platform is designed around three distinct user roles, connecting the station counter, the road, and the customer's doorstep in real time.

---

## 1. Water Refilling Operators (Station Counter)
* **Role:** Manage day-to-day station operations, walk-in and delivery orders, container inventory, and financial accounting.
* **Current Frustrations:** Rewriting customer details, computing totals by hand, digging through paper notebooks to check unpaid balances, and manually recopying sales at closing.
* **What They Need in the App:**
  * **Instant Preset Pricing:** Stored container sizes and prices so bills are never calculated by hand.
  * **Searchable Order & Container Log:** Instant visibility into customer histories, unpaid balances, and how many empty containers are currently out with each customer.
  * **Live Revenue & True Profit Tracking:** Automated deduction of operational costs (water, electricity, container maintenance) from gross revenue, paired with one-tap End-of-Day summary reports.

---

## 2. Delivery Teams (Riders)
* **Role:** Transport refilled water containers to households and offices, collect empty containers, and collect doorstep payments.
* **Current Frustrations:** Guessing which route to take, arriving at locked gates for unscheduled pickups, counting large loads manually, and handwriting delivery slips.
* **What They Need in the App:**
  * **Assigned & Sequenced Routes:** A single turn-by-turn navigation screen that automatically sequences deliveries and empty container pickups on the same street into one trip.
  * **Daily Pickup & Delivery Manifest:** A clear, live list of scheduled stops and time windows for the day.
  * **AI Photo Container Counting:** Ability to snap a single photo of a container load to instantly count and price the order.
  * **One-Tap Proof of Delivery & Smart Receipts:** Quick doorstep verification that issues an official receipt (via Bluetooth thermal printer or digital SMS/in-app copy) tailored to the payment status.

---

## 3. Customers (Households & Offices)
* **Role:** Order water refills, return empty containers, and settle bills.
* **Current Frustrations:** Calling or texting the wrong branch, repeating address and order details every time, waiting without knowing when the rider will arrive, and unclear billing/balance records.
* **What They Need in the App:**
  * **One-Click Ordering:** Saved address profiles and preset container preferences for effortless reordering.
  * **Flexible Pickup Scheduling:** Ability to book specific days and time slots (e.g., `8:00–10:00 AM`, `1:00–3:00 PM`, `4:00–6:00 PM`) for empty container collection, complete with day-before reminders.
  * **Upfront Invoicing & Live Tracking:** Visibility into the exact invoice amount before the rider leaves the station, real-time rider arrival updates, and transparent digital receipts for cash, GCash/Maya, or partial payments.
