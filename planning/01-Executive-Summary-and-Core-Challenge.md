# Executive Summary & Core Challenge

## Project Overview
* **Project Name:** LG Nature's Water — Reimagining Water Delivery Operations
* **Core Objective:** Integrate an all-in-one mobile application to streamline orders, deliveries, container pickups, Point of Sale (POS), and digital/printed receipts.
* **Primary Target:** Achieve a **40% performance growth** in daily station and delivery operations.

> *"How might we integrate a mobile app to streamline orders, deliveries, and container pickups to boost performance by 40%?"*

---

## The Core Problem: Where Performance Leaks
Currently, station operations rely heavily on manual paper notebooks, mental calculations, and memory. Every sale, delivery, and container return is recorded on paper and totaled by hand, causing severe bottlenecks across three operational pillars:

### 1. Manual Order Taking
* **Slow, repetitive encoding:** Requests are written down by hand, one transaction at a time. The exact same customer details (name, address, container preferences) are rewritten for every single order.

### 2. Unplanned Deliveries
* **No route optimization:** Riders leave the water station without a planned route or a live customer list, relying on guesswork to navigate stops.

### 3. Untracked Container Pickups
* **Memory-based tracking:** Empty containers out with customers are tracked from memory rather than structured records, leading to lost assets and uncollected containers.

---

## Key Operational Pain Points

| Pain Point | What Happens Today | Operational Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Repetitive Writing** | Operators handwrite every order, delivery log, and closing summary. | High time spent per transaction; limits how many orders can be processed per shift. |
| **Calculation Mistakes** | Totals, exact change, and daily sales are computed mentally or on a basic calculator. | Frequent pricing errors, revenue leakage, and slow checkout/closing times. |
| **Unsearchable Records** | All historical data lives inside physical paper notebooks. | Nearly impossible to quickly audit order history, verify unpaid customer balances, or track overdue containers. |

---

## Expected Impact (The 40% Performance Case)
By replacing paper notebooks with a unified digital workflow, the time saved on writing and computing is converted directly into serving more orders and collecting more containers within the exact same shift:

* **↓ Less Time per Transaction:** Tap-to-order replaces manual handwriting.
* **↓ Fewer Calculation Errors:** Totals, fees, and balances are computed automatically by the POS.
* **↑ More Trips per Route:** Delivery and pickup stops are sequenced automatically for riders.
